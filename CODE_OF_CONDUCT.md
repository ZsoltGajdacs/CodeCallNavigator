# Conduct in project issues

Keep bug reports, feature requests and comments respectful, relevant and factual.
Disagree with ideas without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
abuse, spam and disclosure of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Zsolt Gajdacs maintains and moderates this project. The maintainer may edit or
remove content, close or lock issues, and block participants at their discretion.
Use GitHub's **Report content** option to report abusive content privately to the
repository owner or GitHub, as available. Do not post private details in an issue
to report misconduct. Moderation is best effort, with no guaranteed response.

This policy applies to public participation in this repository. Participation is
limited to bug reports, feature requests and information clarifying those issues;
see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
